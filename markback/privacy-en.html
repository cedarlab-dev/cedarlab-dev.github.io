Privacy Policy

Markback / Last updated: October 25, 2026

"Markback" ("the App") respects your privacy. The App lets you mark up a screenshot with circles and other marks, add one line of note (by voice or text), and export the result as a folder containing report.md and the annotated PNGs so that an AI coding agent can read it. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Developer Information

  • Developer: Akira Sugiyama (Cedarlab)
  • Location: Japan. As this is an individual developer, detailed address information will be disclosed without delay through the contact email when legally required or reasonably requested.
  • App: Markback
  • Contact: legal@cedarlab.dev

1. The App Does Not Transmit Anything to External Servers

The App never transmits your data to any external server. The App has none of the following:

  • Accounts or sign-in
  • Advertising
  • Analytics
  • Tracking

The developer never receives your data.

2. Information Handled on Your Device

Data saved by the App is stored only on your device, inside the App Group shared container used by the App and its share extension. Main data includes:

  • The name of the target app
  • Imported screenshots
  • Annotations (circles and other marks)
  • The one-line note (transcribed from voice, or typed)
  • Screen names
  • App settings

This information is used to create, view, and edit your findings, and to export the folder you hand to an AI coding agent.

3. Photo Library

Only when you tap "Import screenshots" does the App ask iOS for read permission for your photo library and read the screenshots. The App never writes to your photo library.

If you use iCloud Photos, iOS may fetch the image from Apple's servers in order to obtain the original of the photo you selected. This is a function of iOS and iCloud; it is not a transmission to the developer's servers.

4. Microphone and Speech Recognition

Recording happens only while you hold "Talk", and transcription is performed on-device. Audio data is neither stored nor transmitted; only the transcribed text is placed into the one-line note.

On first use, iOS may download a speech recognition model from Apple for transcription.

5. Share Extension "Add to Markback"

Images shared from other apps are downscaled by the share extension and stored temporarily on your device, then imported when you next open the main app. Shared images are never transmitted externally.

6. Handling of Exported Folders

You choose where "Hand to AI" exports to:

  • iCloud Drive (synced by Apple's iCloud)
  • On My iPhone (in the Files app)
  • A folder you select
  • AirDrop / the share sheet

The exported folder contains report.md and the annotated PNGs. Once exported or sent, the information is handled according to the policy of the destination service (Apple's iCloud, the cloud storage you selected, the receiving app, and so on).

7. In-App Purchase (Apple StoreKit)

Payment for the "One-time unlock" is processed through Apple's StoreKit. The developer does not receive payment information such as credit card numbers. Purchases are restored through your Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's policies.

Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

8. Email Support

If you contact legal@cedarlab.dev or support@cedarlab.dev, the following information may be obtained and processed:

  • Sender email address
  • Name, organization, or similar information included in the sender field or signature
  • Email body, environment information, and attachments such as bug report screenshots

Purpose: replying to inquiries, investigating bugs, and handling privacy-rights requests.

Retention: Support and privacy correspondence is retained for up to 12 months after the inquiry is resolved, then deleted. You may request deletion at any time by replying to the thread or contacting legal@cedarlab.dev.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Data Storage and Deletion

Local data is retained while the app remains installed on your device. Within the App you can delete individual findings, "Handed-off findings", and target apps. Deleting the app deletes the App-related local data on the device.

Folders you have already exported remain at the export destination. If you no longer need them, please delete them yourself from the destination (iCloud Drive, the Files app, the folder you selected, and so on).

11. Rights of Residents in the EEA / UK (GDPR)

The current version of the App is distributed in Japan and the United States. This section applies if distribution is expanded to the EEA / UK in the future or if applicable law requires it.

The data controller for the App is Akira Sugiyama (Cedarlab), an individual developer residing in Japan. To the extent applicable, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, object to processing of, or port your personal data, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, please contact the email address in Section 14. Because the App does not transmit your data to the developer's servers, the only information the developer holds is the content of the support email described in Section 8.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date on this page will be changed. Material changes may also be announced within the App.

13. Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of Japan.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy and data deletion requests should be sent to legal@cedarlab.dev.
