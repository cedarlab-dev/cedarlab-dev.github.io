Support

Markback / Last updated: October 12, 2026

Markback lets you mark up a screenshot with circles and other marks, add one line of note (by voice or text), and export the result with "Hand to AI" as a folder containing report.md and the annotated PNGs. By letting an AI coding agent such as Claude Code or Codex read that folder, you can hand over exactly what you want fixed in your own app.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, please contact:

  • Email: support@cedarlab.dev

We usually reply within 1-3 business days. For bug reports, it helps to include the screen where it happened, the steps you took, the App version, and your iOS version.

FAQ

Q. The exported folder does not show up on my Mac

If you exported to iCloud Drive, it can take a while for iCloud to finish syncing. If it still does not appear after a while, please check:

  • That your iPhone and Mac are signed in with the same Apple ID
  • That iCloud Drive is turned on for both devices
  • That you have a network connection

If you are in a hurry, choose AirDrop when exporting to hand the folder to your Mac without waiting for sync.

Q. How do I hand the folder to Claude Code or Codex?

Once the exported folder is on your Mac, open Claude Code / Codex in the project folder of the app you want to fix, and paste your request. The agent reads report.md and the annotated PNGs in the folder to understand your notes.

Q. How do I restore my purchase?

After changing devices or reinstalling, you can restore Pro Unlock from Settings > Plan > Restore Purchase in the App. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's refund policy. Please request a refund directly via Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

Q. How do I delete my data?

Within the App you can delete individual notes, notes you have handed off, and target apps. Deleting the app removes the App's data from your device.

Folders you have already exported remain at the export destination (iCloud Drive, the Files app, the folder you selected, and so on). If you no longer need them, please delete them there yourself.
