Support

SakuCAD helps with design, printability checks, and file export for home 3D printing.

Get help

Email support@cedarlab.dev. Include the SakuCAD version, operating-system version, what you expected, and the exact steps that produced the problem. Screenshots are useful, but remove API keys, personal names, private paths, and confidential project content first.

Frequently asked questions

Does SakuCAD control my printer?

No. SakuCAD does not slice, generate G-code, send print jobs, or control a printer. Export a 3MF or STL and complete those steps in your slicer. STEP export is also available for CAD exchange.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account or internet connection is required for core CAD, save/open, checks, or export. An internet connection is needed only if you explicitly use a remote AI provider.

Which files can I open?

SakuCAD opens its native project files. Version 1.0 can import SVG or ASCII DXF outlines into a sketch, place PNG or JPEG images as calibrated tracing underlays, and import STL or 3MF meshes as independent or reference geometry. Imported meshes do not regain editable B-Rep history, dimensions, or sketch constraints. STEP is available for export but not import.

Does a green printability check guarantee a successful print?

No. Checks identify selected geometric risks against the current profile; they cannot account for every slicer option, material condition, machine calibration, orientation, or real-world failure. Confirm settings in the slicer and validate on the target printer.

Is AI required?

No. AI assistance is optional. Remote AI requests show what will be sent and require confirmation. Every returned proposal is validated and must be approved in SakuCAD before it can change the document.

Where are licenses and version details?

Open About from the Home or Workspace screen. It includes the app version, safety scope, and third-party notices.
