Support

SakuCAD helps with design, printability checks, and file export for home 3D printing.

Get help

Email support@cedarlab.dev. Include the SakuCAD version, operating-system version, what you expected, and the exact steps that produced the problem. Screenshots are useful, but remove API keys, personal names, private paths, and confidential project content first.

Frequently asked questions

Does SakuCAD control my printer?

No. SakuCAD does not slice, generate G-code, send print jobs, or control a printer. Export a 3MF or STL and complete those steps in your slicer. SakuCAD Pro adds STEP export for CAD exchange.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account or internet connection is required for core CAD, save/open, checks, or 3MF/STL export. The App Store is needed to purchase or restore SakuCAD Pro, and a network connection is needed only while using a remote AI provider.

What is included in Free and SakuCAD Pro?

Free includes templates, sketches, core solid tools, basic placement, Undo and Redo, normal saving, print checks, 3MF/STL export, and validated AI plan import. On Mac, the one-time SakuCAD Pro purchase adds advanced import, sweep and loft, duplicate/pattern/multi-part tools, motion checks, calibration models, durable snapshots, STEP export, direct AI connections, local MCP, and selection-aware iteration.

What happens to a Pro document without Pro access?

It still opens and remains viewable. Free tools remain available when they do not modify or re-enable Pro steps; normal saving and 3MF/STL export remain available. Existing Pro steps can be disabled or removed. Use Restore Purchases from the SakuCAD Pro screen to refresh a previous purchase.

Which files can I open?

SakuCAD opens its native project files. SakuCAD Pro can import SVG or ASCII DXF outlines into a sketch, place PNG or JPEG images as calibrated tracing underlays, and import STL or 3MF meshes as independent or reference geometry. Imported meshes do not regain editable B-Rep history, dimensions, or sketch constraints. STEP is available for Pro export but not import.

Does a green printability check guarantee a successful print?

No. Checks identify selected geometric risks against the current profile; they cannot account for every slicer option, material condition, machine calibration, orientation, or real-world failure. Confirm settings in the slicer and validate on the target printer.

Is AI required?

No. AI assistance is optional. Validated plan import is included in Free. SakuCAD Pro adds direct remote-provider and local MCP connections. Remote requests show what will be sent and require confirmation. Every returned proposal must be approved in SakuCAD before it can change the document.

Where are licenses and version details?

Open About from the Home or Workspace screen. It includes the app version, safety scope, and third-party notices.
