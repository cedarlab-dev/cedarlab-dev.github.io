SakuCAD keeps common actions visible and explains what is needed, while preserving feature history, parametric sketches, and advanced solid tools.

Shape directly

Choose a template or solid, then push or pull a planar face and revise dimensions later.

Build accurately

Use constrained sketches, extrude, revolve, holes, fillets, chamfers, shells, and booleans.

Check before export

Review build volume and printability concerns against a printer profile, then export 3MF or STL.

Start free. Unlock advanced tools once.

The free app includes core modeling, normal project saving, Undo and Redo, print checks, 3MF/STL export, and validated AI plan import. On Mac, SakuCAD Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase.

Advanced modeling

Add sweep, loft, duplicate, mirror, pattern, and multi-part alignment tools.

Import and exchange

Import SVG/DXF, images, and STL/3MF meshes, and export STEP for CAD exchange.

Connected workflows

Use durable snapshots, motion checks, direct AI providers, local MCP, and selection-aware iteration.

Your documents stay yours: Documents containing Pro features still open and remain viewable in Free. Free tools remain available when they do not modify or re-enable Pro steps; normal saving and 3MF/STL export remain available.

AI can propose. You decide.

AI assistance is optional. Free plan import and Pro connections both validate every proposal, show a change list, and require explicit approval before changing the document. Core CAD needs no account or cloud.

Scope: SakuCAD designs, checks, and exports. It does not slice models, generate G-code, or control a printer.
