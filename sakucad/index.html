Clear at the start. Capable as you grow.

SakuCAD keeps common actions visible and explains what is needed, while preserving feature history, parametric sketches, and advanced solid tools.

Shape directly

Choose a template or solid, then push or pull a planar face and revise dimensions later.

Build accurately

Use constrained sketches, extrude, revolve, sweep, loft, fillet, chamfer, shell, booleans, patterns, and mirrors.

Check before export

Review build volume and printability concerns against a printer profile, then export 3MF, STL, or STEP.

AI can propose. You decide.

AI assistance is optional. Every proposal is validated, shown as a change list, and requires explicit approval before it can change the document. Core CAD needs no account or cloud.

Scope: SakuCAD designs, checks, and exports. It does not slice models, generate G-code, or control a printer.
